Vex Robotics Competition – The Unicorn

Using Vex Robotics components, my teammate and I designed, constructed, and programmed a clawbot capable of performing remotely-controlled, user-defined tasks as well as pre-defined autonomous tasks. The robot needed to be capable of picking up balls and placing them into bins, as well as pick up cones and place them on poles. The Unicorn utilized two flat paddles with tension and grip improved using rubber bands, as well as a hammered piece of metal to act as the unicorn horn. The horn was used to move cones around and was the primary reason this robot was so successful.

This small-scale build was entered into a schoolwide Vex Robotics competition for new Mechatronics students. My teammate and I ended up winning first prize in the tournament.

Certifications

Digital Imaging and Remote Sensing Image Generation (DIRSIG) Training Certification

  • Description: The Digital Imaging and Remote Sensing Image Generation (DIRSIG) model is a physics-driven synthetic image generation model developed by the Digital Imaging and Remote Sensing Laboratory at Rochester Institute of Technology. The model can produce passive single-band, multi-spectral or hyper-spectral imagery from the visible through the thermal infrared region of the electromagnetic spectrum. The model also has a very mature active laser (LIDAR) capability and an evolving active RF (RADAR) capability. The model can be used to test image system designs, to create test imagery for evaluating image exploitation algorithms and for creating data for training image analysts.

Siemens Digital Industry Academy - Basics of PLC Certification

  • Description: Siemens basic PLC certification focuses on foundational knowledge of SIMATIC controllers and TIA Portal software, covering hardware configuration, programming (Ladder Logic/SCL), and troubleshooting

Amateur Radio License - Technician Class

  • Description: The Technician Class license is the entry-level amateur radio license in the U.S., requiring a 35-question multiple-choice exam on basic regulations, electronics, and safety. It grants full privileges on all VHF/UHF bands (above 30 MHz) and limited privileges on HF (shortwave) bands.
